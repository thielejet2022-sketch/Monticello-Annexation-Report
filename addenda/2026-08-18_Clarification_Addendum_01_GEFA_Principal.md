# Clarification Addendum No. 1

**Report:** Mine Lake Investigative Report  
**Subject:** FY2019 GEFA principal reduction and previously described unexplained variance  
**Issued:** August 18, 2026  
**Status:** Corrective clarification; the original report remains unchanged

## Purpose

This addendum corrects the report's characterization of an approximately $89,000 amount as unexplained. A subsequent loan-level reconciliation of the City's FY2018 and FY2019 audited financial statements, considered together with the March 28, 2019 City Council minutes, shows that the financial records are consistent with the return of $90,661.32 in unspent Mine Lake funds to the Georgia Environmental Finance Authority (GEFA).

## Corrected Finding

The FY2018 audit reports an outstanding GEFA loan balance of **$3,267,486** at June 30, 2018. The FY2019 audit reports an outstanding balance of **$3,037,641** at June 30, 2019. The year-over-year reduction is therefore:

| Reconciliation | Amount |
|---|---:|
| GEFA balance at June 30, 2018 | $3,267,486.00 |
| Less: GEFA balance at June 30, 2019 | ($3,037,641.00) |
| Total FY2019 GEFA principal reduction | **$229,845.00** |
| Less: Council-authorized return/credit | ($90,661.32) |
| Implied ordinary FY2019 principal payments | **$139,183.68** |

The FY2018 audit's debt-service schedule independently identifies **$229,845** as GEFA principal due during FY2019. The FY2019 Water and Sewer Fund cash-flow statement reports **$422,368** in aggregate principal payments on debt. That broader total includes the GEFA reduction as well as principal paid on other Water and Sewer obligations.

Accordingly, the $90,661.32 return was not separately labeled in the audited statements, but it appears to have been incorporated into the GEFA principal reduction. The available audited balances support the conclusion that the Council-authorized return was financially recognized.

## Superseded Language

Any statement in the original report identifying an approximately $89,000 amount as an unexplained GEFA or Mine Lake variance should be treated as withdrawn and superseded by this addendum. The separate **$89,191 MEAG note balance** reported at June 30, 2019 should not be conflated with the GEFA transaction.

## Remaining Verification Item

The audits establish the balance-level reconciliation but do not separately identify the transaction date, payment instrument, or GEFA's description of the $90,661.32 adjustment. Those details should be confirmed through the City's general ledger, bank records, and GEFA's borrower ledger for loan DW2017021. This remaining verification item does not alter the corrected conclusion above.

---

**Effect of addendum:** This correction is limited to the characterization of the approximately $89,000 variance. It does not modify the report's other findings or conclusions.
