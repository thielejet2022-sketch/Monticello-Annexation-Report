# Repository Instructions

## Evidence rules

- Preserve issued reports. Do not silently rewrite a published finding.
- Correct a material error through a separately dated addendum linked to the affected report.
- Label statements as documented fact, inference, allegation, opinion, or unresolved question when the distinction matters.
- Cite the primary record whenever available. Identify the document title, date, page or section, and source URL or evidence-index identifier.
- Do not describe an amount as missing or unexplained until relevant beginning balances, ending balances, additions, reductions, transfers, and aggregated obligations have been reconciled.
- Record contrary evidence and meaningful limitations.

## Public-repository safeguards

- Do not commit Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, credentials, private correspondence, personal contact information, privileged material, or unreviewed ORA productions.
- Use a source index and SHA-256 hash when a source should be authenticated but not publicly distributed.
- Treat working hypotheses as nonfinal and keep them out of issued reports until supported.

## Change control

- Use a dedicated branch and pull request for substantive report, addendum, or evidence-index changes.
- Explain what changed, why it changed, the supporting evidence, and the validation performed.
- Update `CHANGELOG.md` for every issued report or addendum.
- Prefer ISO dates (`YYYY-MM-DD`) in filenames and records.
